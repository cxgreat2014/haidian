KM0 · Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Proposal

KM0 (Kilometer Zero) is the master concept: in 1909, China's first self-engineered railway began its survey from the Jing-Zhang line; today, innovation in the age of AI restarts its count at a new Kilometer Zero beside the historic Qinghuayuan Station. The proposal runs a 9.6 km 'Meridian' spine through three stations and two wings, delivers recomputable land-use geometry and metrics, 12 AI scenario cards, 4 pilgrimage landmarks, and an annual operations system. All spatial content is a conceptual proposal for professional teams to deepen, not an approved planning conclusion.

KM0 · Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Proposal

Name: KM0 · Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt (零公里 · 百年京张AI创新带) Slogan: Recount From Here. / 从这里，重新计数。 One-line positioning: Turn the 9-kilometre urban rift left by the Jing-Zhang Railway into the "mileage origin" of global AI innovation — a first mile of the intelligent age that can be walked, recomputed from evidence, and pilgrimaged by developers worldwide.

In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway — engineered under Zhan Tianyou — was completed: the first trunk railway surveyed, designed, and built by Chinese engineers. A century later, the Jing-Zhang high-speed railway dives underground through the Qinghuayuan Tunnel, returning the surface to the city. This proposal installs a conceptual "New Kilometer Zero" monument beside the former Qinghuayuan Station site: mileage in 1909 was measured from there; innovation today is recounted from here. This does not rewrite the historic origin (the railway's zero point is recorded variously as Liucun or Xizhimen and awaits authoritative confirmation) — it is a deliberate ceremonial act: every metre of the innovation belt receives a citable chainage.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Industry reading: Haidian already stands at the counting origin

Haidian's core AI industry reached RMB 282.2 billion in 2024 and reportedly exceeded 350 billion in 2026, about 30% of the national total; it hosts 1,900+ AI companies (about 80% of Beijing's), ~130 filed foundation models, and ~12,300 AI scholars Source. In March 2026 the district published its "1+X+1" modern industry system with AI at the apex Source, and at the 2026 Zhongguancun Forum it announced the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt with a "three zones, two wings" structure Source. Our judgment: Haidian does not lack industrial density — it lacks a city system that makes this density perceptible, participable, and memorable. That is the question for the 43.6 km² research area Depth.

Master concept and naming system (Task agent.1)

Naming rationale. Candidate systems were scored across cultural depth, international legibility, and systemic extensibility. The Jing-Zhang story is entirely about measurement — measuring mountains (the herringbone switchback climbing the Guangou gorge at 33‰), measuring national capability (the first self-built trunk railway), measuring eras (the 2019 smart high-speed line diving underground). The fitting tribute from an AI-designed innovation belt is to start counting again Source Source.

  • Slogan: Recount From Here. / 从这里，重新计数。

  • Naming-system extensions (conceptual, for professional deepening):

The palette: rail teal, sleeper brown, signal red, steam white. Wordmarks should be customised from open-source fonts (Source Han family / monospace for chainage numbers); all fonts and graphic elements must clear licensing checks before use Standard.
  • Three-zones-two-wings synergy loop: Origin Station supplies original innovation and talent; Acceleration Station delivers full-stack R&D and pilot scale-up; Departure Station achieves productisation and consumer conversion; the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing feeds capital and professional services from the west; the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing supplies test scenarios and urban data from the east. People flow north–south along the Meridian; factors flow east–west along the seams — an innovation loop, not a linear division Source.

Future city form

Our judgment: the urban form that fits AI's new productive forces is not "a smarter business park" but three operating traits of an evolvable city (conceptual): first, conditional morphology — height, massing, and interface are written as functions of official controls, heritage sight-lines, and rail-safety setbacks, recomputed automatically when data arrives (our geometry engine already works this way); second, space as a service — long-term holding, graded leasing, and open scenarios, after Station F and Punggol Source Source; third, public legitimacy first — Sidewalk Toronto proved that a smart district without public legitimacy gets rejected by the city, so this proposal puts data-governance architecture ahead of any sensor deployment Source.

Acceleration Station — Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent-Innovation Acceleration Area (K+1+769 to K+2+339; announced ≈ 192.1 ha)

  • Positioning: a garden-type AI innovation district serving the full-stack independent innovation system and AI-governance voice. Public reports describe Zhongzhiyuan (the Zhongguancun Dongsheng Science Park cluster at the north end of Xueyuan Road) as focusing on embodied AI, AI+cultural content, and AI+health, opening in late 2026, with Changping-line Xuezhiyuan Station connected underground Source.
  • Spatial structure: a "green valley + courtyards" skeleton — a garden research valley at the centre ringed by full-stack AI courtyards; the Qing River waterfront to the north carries ecology and buffered outdoor testing; the 5th-Ring gateway plaza and the valley test ground form the external interface.
  • Programme and scenarios: concept courtyards for the full stack (chips–frameworks–models–standards), reserve capacity linked to national platforms (via strategic reserve), the embodied-AI outdoor test field (SC-10), and the digital-twin proving ground (SC-08); Qing River culture is told through waterfront narrative nodes.
  • Transport: conceptual station integration at the Xuezhiyuan micro-centre and Qinghe Station hub; only principled suggestions are made for 5th-Ring integration — engineering remains with official studies.
  • Delivery risk: existing inventory and ownership are not public; early leasing strength must not be written as commitment; the tension between garden-scale intensity and industry scale is left to regulatory deepening.

Origin Station — Beijing AI Origin Community (K-0+997 to K0; announced ≈ 104.3 ha)

  • Positioning: a campus-adjacent AI innovation district — the first choice for innovation sourcing and entrepreneurship. Public reports count 400+ companies with ~74% in AI; Dongsheng Tower has been renamed Origin Tower (~138,900 m²); the community entered Beijing's first list of AI innovation blocks in January 2026 Source Source.
  • Spatial structure: a public skeleton of "K0 Origin Square + open-source release gallery + talent living rooms", linking the Qinghuayuan memory node (K0+000, site of the New Kilometer Zero monument) to the north and Wudaokou station forecourt to the south. Concept land use prioritises education interfaces, incubation, and talent apartments, with low-disturbance organic renewal — functional replacement and micro-renovation, never wholesale demolition.
  • Programme and scenarios: technology-transfer incubation, the open-source release gallery (SC-11), the enterprise-service copilot (SC-05), and international-fellow services; campus interfaces with Tsinghua, Peking University, and CAS institutes follow a "wall-less campus" concept (after SIT's embedding into Punggol) Source.
  • Transport: conceptual station integration at Wudaokou (Line 13) and Qinghua Donglu Xikou (Line 15); campus–park slow links run along the seams and greenway.
  • Delivery risk: university and park ownership is complex — every interface intervention presumes owner consent; talent housing must align with affordable-housing policy and guard against gentrification displacement (the Barcelona superblock lesson) Source.

Departure Station — Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (K-6+109 to K-5+453; announced ≈ 72.0 ha)

  • Positioning: an urban-type AI innovation district — the source of AI-native business species and content consumption. The area hosts ByteDance's most concentrated Beijing office cluster (Fangheng Plaza, Zhongkun Plaza, 1733, and others), and in February 2026 ByteDance acquired the Lanjing Lijia plot for a planned digital-economy park; Metro Line 12 (opened December 2024) interchanges with Line 13 here Source.
  • Spatial structure: organised around "Zhihui Plaza + four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity" — a demonstration of pedestrian links across the four quadrants of the Dazhongsi station junction (SN-08), with concept paths tying the station to key plots; the AI-native consumption experience field (SN-02) extends along the seam.
  • Programme and scenarios: agents, smart terminals, content consumption, and a data-element circulation sandbox (SC-09); public-realm upgrades around leading companies use "street living rooms + cycling micro-circulation"; planned green space is compounded for sports and markets.
  • Cultural layer: Juesheng Temple (Dazhongsi, imperially founded 1733, housing the Yongle Great Bell, a national protected monument) is the area's deepest time layer — bell and milepost together anchor Departure Station's identity (no construction is imagined inside protection boundaries) Source.
  • Delivery risk: station integration and quadrant connectivity involve engineering and ownership and remain conceptual; commercial upgrading must respect incumbent enterprises' intentions.

Land-use layout

Building scale and conditional morphology

With no public existing-building inventory, this proposal offers no existing-versus-planned conclusions, only concept quantities: ~790,000 m² of concept footprints (769 concept buildings), concept building coverage ≈ 6.9%, and a concept gross-floor-area magnitude of ≈ 5.22 million m² Metric Metric Metric. Their legal status is design-study quantities: they test whether the spatial organisation can host the industrial magnitude; they are not filed indicators.

Form control uses conditional rules, not numeric conclusions (conceptual): concept height ceiling = f(official height control, heritage/landscape sight-lines, rail-safety setback, airport clearance) — with official data missing, the function deliberately outputs no number; we state only two principles ("low along the rail spine, mid-rise along streets, taller at station nodes") and two research scenarios (conservative / aspirational), to be recomputed once controls are published Depth Depth.

Retain–renovate–demolish logic

Concept buildings carry zone-specific conceptual retain/renovate/new ratios (Dazhongsi ≈ 55/35/10; Origin Community ≈ 50/35/15; Zhongzhiyuan ≈ 15/30/55). The rule: heritage and active innovation stock is retained or renovated first; new capacity concentrates in garden districts and underused plots. This classification demonstrates strategy, not decisions; before ownership, building-condition surveys, and resident consultation exist, no specific parcel enters a demolish decision Depth Source.
